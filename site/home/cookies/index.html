Cookies Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · SCIMA Learning (study.scima-net.com)

We do not use cookies

SCIMA Learning sets no cookies whatsoever — not first-party, not third-party, not for analytics, advertising or tracking. There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What we use instead: browser local storage

The app is local-first: it stores your work in your browser’s own storage so it survives reloads and works offline. This is functionally similar to a cookie but never transmitted to us:

  • mf_state — decks, folders, cards, review schedule, settings, themes and the Crucible adaptive profile.
  • SCIMATracker — XP, streaks, marks and analytics history.
  • scima_* keys — page memory, stored book chapters/PDF data and blobs belonging to your library.
  • sessionStorage — short-lived view memory, discarded when the tab closes.

This storage is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (a study app that remembers your cards); without it the app cannot function.

Third parties

Pages request the Nunito typeface from Google Fonts; that request is governed by Google’s own privacy practices and may involve cookies on Google’s domain, which we neither set nor read. Define/Translate lookups contact Google Translate and dictionaryapi.dev only when you perform a lookup.

Control

You can inspect and delete everything the app stores at any time: Settings → Data (export / import / destroy), or your browser’s “clear site data” for study.scima-net.com. Clearing it removes your decks from this browser permanently — export first if you want a copy.
