Privacy Policy — SCIMA Learning

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · SCIMA Learning (study.scima-net.com)

The short version

SCIMA Learning is a local-first study app. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your decks, flashcards, review history, analytics, timing data and adaptive learning profile are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are never uploaded to us. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and we set no cookies at all.

Data that never leaves your device

  • Decks, folders, cards, images you attach, and imported books/PDFs (parsed entirely in your browser).
  • Review sessions, ratings, answer timings, hesitancy and the Crucible adaptive-difficulty profile (including the weak-word map).
  • Analytics, streaks, XP, quests, settings and themes.

You can export or erase all of it at any time from Settings → Data (export/import/destroy), or by clearing site data in your browser.

Data that can leave your device, and only when you ask for it

  • Community deck registry. If you publish a deck, its name, description, author name you chose, tags and card content are stored on our server so others can find and download it. Downloads and publishes are rate-limited and logged minimally (see below). If you have published decks and want one removed, contact us and we will delete it.
  • Define / Translate. The term you look up is sent to Google’s public translate endpoint and to dictionaryapi.dev to return a definition or translation. No other context is sent.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Nunito typeface from Google Fonts; as with any web font request, your IP address and browser user-agent are visible to that CDN when a page loads.
  • Local AI Capture. If you configure a local LLM endpoint, text you generate from is sent only to that endpoint on your own machine/network — never to us.

Server-side logs & infrastructure

The site is served behind a CDN (Cloudflare). The CDN and our web server keep standard, short-lived infrastructure logs (IP address, request path, user-agent) for security and abuse prevention. The community registry additionally stores the publish/download metadata described above. We do not combine server logs with your local app data — we have no account system to link them with.

Children

SCIMA Learning is designed for secondary-school students and requires no registration and no personal details. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone; the only content we store is what a user deliberately publishes to the community registry, which can be created without any real name.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of any personal data we hold (in practice: registry entries and the minimal logs above), and to object to processing. Because everything else lives in your browser, erasure there is entirely under your control. To exercise any right against data we hold, email scima.main@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date, and summarised in the app changelog.
